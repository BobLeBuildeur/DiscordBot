# Opinionated Simplicity (Underrated)

Default to the simplest thing that works. Avoid premature microservices, event overengineering, and complex abstractions; if you do not have scale pain yet, do not solve it.

## What it means

- Avoid premature microservices.
- Avoid event overengineering.
- Avoid complex abstractions.

## Rule of thumb

If you don't have scale pain yet, don't solve it.
