# Work Stream 08: Go to Market

**Deliverable:** Go-to-market strategy, positioning, pricing, launch plan, and initial customer engagement.

---

## Vision Context

The platform's value proposition is clear: **Everyone becomes the manager of their own agentic workforce.** Users shift from manual execution to agent supervision.

**Target users:** Analysts working in Excel- and PowerPoint-style environments—people who today spend hours on analytical and reporting tasks that could be planned, supervised, and refined by AI agents.

**Key differentiators:**
- Plan → Revise → Build workflow (user stays in control)
- Books (composable institutional knowledge)
- Excel-like table interface (familiar, powerful)
- Analysis → Communication (tables to slides)

The GTM strategy must communicate this transformation and reach the right users at the right time.

## Positioning (Detailed)

### Value Proposition

**Headline:** "Everyone becomes the manager of their own agentic workforce."

**Supporting points:**
1. **Plan** — Describe your task; the system proposes a structured plan (steps, data, methods, outputs).
2. **Revise** — Review and edit before execution. Natural language, table manipulation, constraints.
3. **Build** — Agents execute the full plan. Tables, charts, slides. Production-grade.

**Proof points:**
- Time saved per report (e.g., "80% faster monthly reports")
- Consistency (Books encode organizational standards)
- Scale (one analyst supervises multiple agent workflows)

### Competitive Positioning

| vs. | Differentiation |
|-----|------------------|
| **Manual Excel/PowerPoint** | Agents do the work; user supervises. No more copy-paste, manual aggregation. |
| **Generic AI assistants** | Plan → Revise → Build is structured. Books provide org context. Not one-off prompts. |
| **BI tools** | Analysis + communication. Tables and slides. Not just dashboards. |

---

## Target Segment (Detailed)

### Primary: Analysts

- **Profile:** Power users of Excel and PowerPoint. Create reports, analyze data, build presentations.
- **Pain:** Repetitive tasks, manual data pulls, inconsistent definitions, time spent on formatting.
- **Gain:** Supervise agents instead of executing. Reusable Books. Fast iteration.

### Secondary: Knowledge Admins

- **Profile:** Define processes, metrics, standards. Often in ops, finance, or central teams.
- **Pain:** Knowledge scattered; agents (or people) don't follow standards.
- **Gain:** Books scale expertise. One source of truth for agents.

### Pilot Industries

| Industry | Use Cases |
|----------|-----------|
| **Finance** | Monthly close, variance analysis, board decks |
| **Sales Ops** | Pipeline reports, quota tracking, territory analysis |
| **Operations** | KPI dashboards, capacity planning, performance reviews |

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

- Mid to large enterprise (500+ employees)
- Centralized analytics or ops team
- Existing data warehouse or BI stack
- Budget for productivity tools
- Willingness to encode knowledge (Books)

## Pricing Model (Options)

| Model | Pros | Cons |
|------|------|------|
| **Seat-based** | Predictable; simple | May under-monetize heavy users |
| **Usage-based** | Fair; scales with value | Complexity; cost uncertainty for buyer |
| **Hybrid** | Base seats + usage overage | More complex to explain |

**Suggested tiers:**
- **Free trial** — Limited plans, 1 project, sample data
- **Team** — N seats, unlimited plans, connected data sources
- **Enterprise** — Custom seats, SSO, Books governance, SLA

## Launch Plan (Phased)

### Phase 1: Private Beta

- **Criteria:** 10–20 design partners. Invite-only.
- **Goal:** Validate workflow, gather feedback, fix critical issues.
- **Support:** High-touch. Weekly check-ins.
- **Duration:** 6–8 weeks.

### Phase 2: Limited GA

- **Criteria:** Beta feedback incorporated. Core workflow stable. Support playbook ready.
- **Goal:** Broader adoption. Refine onboarding. Measure activation.
- **Support:** Standard. Documentation, community, ticket-based.
- **Duration:** 8–12 weeks.

### Phase 3: Full GA

- **Criteria:** N active users, retention curve positive, no critical bugs.
- **Goal:** Scale acquisition. Full marketing and sales motion.
- **Support:** Tiered (community, standard, premium).
